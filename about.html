About

Your partner in financial clarity.

With years of hands-on experience, we deliver reliable, detail-oriented bookkeeping and payroll support tailored to your business needs.

Mitzi Ivy Arbollente, Certified Bookkeeper

With 9 years of experience in bookkeeping and accounting support, Mitzi's career began in the corporate environment with Nissan Philippines, where she spent three years building a strong foundation in financial processes and accounting operations. She later transitioned into the offshore industry, working with businesses internationally, and over the past six years has specialized in full-cycle bookkeeping across diverse industries.

Mitzi founded MIA Bookkeeping and Co. to bring that experience together and build a bookkeeping practice dedicated to supporting small and growing businesses — giving business owners dependable financial support so they can focus on what they do best: running and growing their business.
