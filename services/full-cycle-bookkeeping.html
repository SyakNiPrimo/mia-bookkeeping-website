Full-Cycle Bookkeeping

We take care of your day-to-day bookkeeping and keep your financial records accurate, organized, and up to date.

Good bookkeeping is more than recording income and expenses. Your books should be accurate, organized, reconciled, and complete so you have a clear picture of how your business is performing.

At MIA Bookkeeping & Co., we provide full-cycle bookkeeping support tailored to your business's needs. Whether you have a growing company with increasing transaction volume or an established business with more complex books, we work to keep your financial records current and properly maintained.

Our bookkeeping services may include:

  • Day-to-day transaction categorization and review
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations
  • Accounts payable and vendor management
  • Accounts receivable and customer account tracking
  • Loan and liability account reconciliations
  • Payroll recording and reconciliation
  • Balance sheet and Profit & Loss review
  • General ledger review and account cleanup
  • Chart of Accounts organization and maintenance
  • Month-end and year-end bookkeeping
  • Financial reports, including Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss
  • Supporting schedules and documentation
  • Coordination with your CPA or tax professional when needed

We work remotely using cloud-based accounting and document-sharing systems, keeping your books organized and accessible wherever your business is located.

Our goal is to handle the bookkeeping from start to finish, so you have reliable financial records and can spend more time managing your business.
